Gowun Batang Bold faces (GBATB*.SVF, GBA*X?.SVF)
================================================

Bitmap faces rasterised from Gowun Batang Bold by mkfont.py (2
bpp anti-aliased). Each is a Modified Version (a
rasterised subset) of the font. Copyright 2021 The Gowun Batang Project
Authors (https://github.com/yangheeryu/Gowun-Batang). SIL Open Font License
1.1: OFLGOWUN.TXT.

  GBATB14.SVF   16x16 2 bpp precomposed, 2446 glyphs; ASCII + the 2,350 KS X
                1001 syllables
  GBATB17.SVF   16x16 2 bpp precomposed, 3155 glyphs; ASCII + KS X 1001 (no
                Hanja), the shared SCI face
  GBATB17B.SVF  26x24 2 bpp precomposed, 2446 glyphs; ASCII + the 2,350
                syllables
  GBATB17O.SVF  20x20 2 bpp precomposed, 1231 glyphs; outline: the 17 px
                face's glyphs dilated by 2 px (a text outline)
  GBATB18.SVF   18x18 2 bpp precomposed, 942 glyphs; subset: the characters of tools/build/cps/<face>.txt
  GBATB18A.SVF  18x18 2 bpp precomposed, 1216 glyphs; subset: the characters of tools/build/cps/<face>.txt
  GBATB21.SVF   22x22 2 bpp precomposed, 1216 glyphs; subset: the characters of tools/build/cps/<face>.txt
  GBATB23.SVF   24x24 2 bpp precomposed, 2446 glyphs; ASCII + the 2,350
                syllables
  GBATB23A.SVF  26x24 2 bpp precomposed, 2446 glyphs; ASCII + the 2,350
                syllables
  GBATB31.SVF   32x32 2 bpp precomposed, 1121 glyphs; subset: the characters of tools/build/cps/<face>.txt
  GBATB35.SVF   36x36 2 bpp precomposed, 2446 glyphs; ASCII + the 2,350
                syllables
  GBATB47.SVF   46x54 2 bpp precomposed, 2446 glyphs; ASCII + the 2,350
                syllables
  GBA14X2.SVF   16x16 2 bpp precomposed, 4 glyphs; supplement of GBATB14
  GBA17BX1.SVF  26x24 2 bpp precomposed, 3 glyphs; supplement of GBATB17B
  GBA23X2.SVF   24x24 2 bpp precomposed, 4 glyphs; supplement of GBATB23
  GBA23AX2.SVF  26x24 2 bpp precomposed, 4 glyphs; supplement of GBATB23A

Names: <font><px>[variant].SVF. <px> is the size the face was drawn at; a
letter after it tells apart faces of the same font and size (another cell,
bit depth or character set). A name ending in X<n> is a supplement: the few
characters a base face lacks, drawn to follow it in a chain. The file names
are abbreviations, not a Reserved Font Name, and the faces carry no font name
inside: they are Modified Versions under the SIL OFL 1.1 that do not use the
Reserved Font Names of the original. How each face is made: tools/build/faces.tsv
and docs/recipes.md.
